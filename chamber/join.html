NP Membership Benefits

  • No cost
  • Basic listing with organization name, address, 25-word mission blurb, and up to two social links.
  • Access to two nonprofit-specific workshops annually (e.g., grant writing, volunteer management).
  • Two complimentary tickets to community networking events per year; discounted fees for additional tickets.

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Business listing with small logo, 50-word description, and two social links.
  • Access to monthly member-only starter workshops and webinars (recordings included).
  • Annual 1-hour marketing strategy session plus one custom 1-page promotional graphic or social asset.

Silver Membership Benefits

  • Additional visibility and promotional perks.
  • Weekly featured position in the directory carousel (12 weeks per year).
  • One month of a priority homepage banner placement every quarter (4 months/year).
  • Two complimentary premium event passes per quarter and priority seating on sponsored events.
  • Quarterly 60-minute strategy sessions with a Chamber advisor focused on business growth and marketing.

Gold Membership Benefits

  • Premium visibility and hands-on support.
  • A premium directory page with up to 250 words, a gallery of three images, and embedded contact or booking links.
  • One month of a priority homepage banner placement every quarter (4 months/year).
  • Twice-yearly e-newsletter highlight sent to the membership list (includes logo, link, and 150-word profile).
  • Invitations to exclusive roundtables and policy briefings, and up to 6 VIP passes per year to premium events.
  • Annual 1-hour marketing strategy session plus one custom 1-page promotional graphic or social asset.
  • Assigned partnership manager for sponsorship opportunities, event coordination, and promotional campaigns.
