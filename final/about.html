Who We Are

Altitude Tours was created to share the magic of La Paz, Bolivia with the world. We are a team of adventure lovers, storytellers, and local experts dedicated to crafting authentic travel experiences. Every tour we design is built to connect visitors with breathtaking landscapes, rich traditions, and the warm spirit of our people.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver unforgettable journeys that inspire travelers to explore, learn, and appreciate the cultural and natural beauty of Bolivia. We collaborate with local guides, small businesses, and rural communities to promote responsible tourism that benefits everyone.

Our Vision

We aim to become the leading adventure platform in La Paz, showcasing the wonders of the Andes while encouraging sustainable travel. We want every visitor to leave with not only amazing photos, but also a deep connection to the history, culture, and energy of our region.

Our Values

  • 🌎 Sustainability: We protect the environment and promote responsible travel.
  • 🤝 Community: We support local businesses and empower Bolivian talent.
  • 🧭 Adventure: We believe in experiences that spark curiosity and excitement.
  • 🛡️ Safety: Your well-being is our top priority on every tour.
  • ❤️ Authenticity: We showcase the true essence of La Paz without filters.

Meet the Team

Behind Altitude Tours is a passionate team committed to creating meaningful travel experiences. From expert guides to dedicated coordinators, each member brings their knowledge and love for Bolivia to ensure every tour is unforgettable. We believe great adventures start with people who truly care.
